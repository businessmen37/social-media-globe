Frequently asked questions

Should I choose Shopify or Amazon?+

They support different business models. We start with your products, how you want to sell and the responsibilities you can manage, then help define the tools and steps that fit your plan.

Do I need an existing business?+

No. You can approach us with an idea or an operating project. Understanding your current position helps us avoid proposing a setup that exceeds your needs.

Can services be combined?+

Yes. A project may connect a website, professional email, AI videos and social content. We define what is included and what is outside the agreed scope.
