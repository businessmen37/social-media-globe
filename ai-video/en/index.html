SOCIAL MEDIA GLOBE

AI video with a clear message and a practical plan.

You need more than another video. You need a message that fits your brand, a format suited to your platform and a process you can understand. Social Media Globe connects AI video creation, editing and automation to your project’s goals.

Discuss your project ↗
01

Create video with artificial intelligence

We begin with your idea: who you want to reach, what you want to communicate and which action the video should support. Then we define the visual direction and the elements needed for production.

A project may use AI-generated visuals, material you supply or a combination. Conceptual imagery is not presented as evidence of client results. Photos, logos, voices and other supplied materials need appropriate permission for the intended use.

02

Editing for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts

Short-form video needs an understandable message and a presentation that works on a phone. Depending on the agreed project, editing can address sequence selection, pacing, on-screen text and message structure.

Share your target platform and existing material. Language, duration, aspect ratio, sound, voice and required versions should be defined in advance. We do not guarantee views, followers or monetization results.

03

Automation for a repeatable content workflow

If you repeat the same content tasks, we help identify steps that can be simplified. This may involve preparing assets, organizing production stages and moving an idea toward a version ready for review.

Automation does not remove the need for human review. We define checkpoints, tools, access and limitations together. We do not require unrestricted access or assume that a system can publish without agreed rules.

04

What to prepare for an AI video project

Share your goal, audience, target platform and examples of styles you like. Tell us whether you already have a logo, product images, footage or copy that can serve as a starting point.

Before production, we clarify what must be created or edited, the number of versions, review arrangements and delivery formats. There is no single fixed price or timeline that fits every project.

05

Content for Haitian audiences and multilingual projects

If your project serves a Haitian audience, tell us which language your intended viewers use and the context in which they will watch. A product introduction, a service explanation and a brand presentation need different message structures. We use that context to discuss the content direction.

For a project involving Haitian Creole, French, English or Spanish, the script language, voice, brand-name pronunciation and any subtitles need to be agreed before production. Share wording that must be retained and any previously approved material. A style reference can help explain your preferences, but it does not replace a message tailored to your own brand.

06

Frequently asked questions

Do you only make fully AI-generated videos?+

No. A project can combine your existing material with AI-generated elements. We choose the approach around the message and the agreed scope.

Can I request videos for TikTok, Reels or Shorts?+

Yes. Tell us the target platform so we can discuss format, duration and required material as part of the project scope.

Does automation include automatic publishing?+

Not by default. We define which steps are automated and which require your review or approval. Publishing and access must be agreed beforehand.
